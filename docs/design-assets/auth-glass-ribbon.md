An original ImageGen raster asset for the user-selected third authentication concept, generated 2026-10-09. No borrowed logo or interface bitmap is included.

Prompt direction: transparent pale rose/lilac broad curved glass ribbons crossing along the bottom and right edge, soft fine specular edges, empty upper-left sector; no text, UI or background. The asset was inspected before use.

`apps/web/public/images/auth-glass-ribbon.webp`: 1254 × 1254 RGBA, 134,556 bytes. It is decorative (`alt=""`, hidden from assistive technology), uses explicit intrinsic dimensions and direct image serving to avoid the known Preview Services optimizer issue. It has no animation, canvas, blur dependency or third-party request. Smaller viewports lower its opacity and keep it outside the form. Theme and action colors continue to come from semantic tokens.
